Future deck · Saturday, July 22, 2028

Basic Info

  • Date: Saturday, July 22, 2028. This is an eclipse-planning deck; travel dates and a viewing site have not been chosen.
  • What happens: The Moon completely covers the Sun within a narrow path across the Indian Ocean, Australia, the Tasman Sea, and New Zealand. Outside that corridor, the eclipse is only partial.
  • Maximum duration: About 5 minutes 10 seconds near the Kimberley region of Western Australia; local duration depends on the observing coordinates. NASA path and duration table.
  • Season: Southern Hemisphere winter. Plan for dry-season travel in northern Australia, cold inland nights, cool Sydney weather, and cold, potentially snowy conditions in southern New Zealand. Weather at any chosen site remains uncertain.
Choose a place inside totality. A nearby city name is not enough: check the exact accommodation and viewing coordinates on the National Solar Observatory interactive map. Planning

  • Site selection: Compare duration, cloud prospects, transport, terrain, and the ability to change location. Do not treat average weather as a clear-sky promise.
  • Reservations: Check accommodation cancellation terms and confirm any eclipse-specific viewing access in writing. This deck does not claim package availability.
  • Local context: Add city-specific history, attractions, holidays, and travel logistics when the destination and stay dates are chosen.

Eye safety

Use undamaged ISO 12312-2-compliant eclipse viewers whenever any bright part of the Sun is visible. Remove them only during full totality at a location inside the path, and replace them before the Sun reappears. Ordinary sunglasses are unsafe. Cameras, binoculars, and telescopes need suitable front-mounted solar filters; eclipse glasses alone do not protect someone looking through optics. NASA viewing guidance.
