Basic Info

The eclipse is on Monday 2 August 2027. Totality crosses parts of southern Spain and northern Africa; the exact viewing coordinates matter. A hotel elsewhere in the same country can be outside totality. Use the astronomical path and local predictions, rather than a tour’s generic country label.

August brings strong sun and high heat, especially inland and in Egypt. Dry climatology improves the odds of clear skies but is not a weather guarantee; coastal marine cloud and desert dust can matter. Ask about shade, water, air conditioning, toilets, viewing access and repositioning. The supplied Space.com booking guide and Responsible Travel Egypt guide explain the viewing and travel considerations.

Selection rule: Included departures explicitly publish remaining places/available status or expose a priced, selectable package or cabin in a booking flow, with no unresolved sold-out conflict. Enquiry forms, headline prices and directory listings alone do not establish space. Provider-reported limited allocations are labelled as such. Available options remain in every country. Prices and reviews: Prices retain the operator’s currency and are normally per person sharing, unless stated otherwise. Small samples, old reports, operator-hosted selections and uncertain hotel/vessel matches are identified.

Two couples: “Four people, two rooms” means four adults in two private double/twin rooms or cabins, two per room. A published place count or traveler selector does not establish two-room stock.
