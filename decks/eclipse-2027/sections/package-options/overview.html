Basic Info

The eclipse is on Monday 2 August 2027. Totality crosses parts of southern Spain and northern Africa; the exact viewing coordinates matter. A hotel elsewhere in the same country can be outside totality. Use the astronomical path and local predictions, rather than a tour’s generic country label.

August brings strong sun and high heat, especially inland and in Egypt. Dry climatology improves the odds of clear skies but is not a weather guarantee; coastal marine cloud and desert dust can matter. Ask about shade, water, air conditioning, toilets, viewing access and repositioning. The supplied Space.com booking guide and Responsible Travel Egypt guide explain the viewing and travel considerations.

Selection rule: Included departures explicitly publish remaining places/available status or expose a priced, selectable package or cabin in a booking flow, with no unresolved sold-out conflict. Enquiry forms, headline prices and directory listings alone do not establish space. Provider-reported limited allocations are labelled as such. Available options were found in every country, so waitlists are kept only in the separate recheck record.

Prices and reviews: Prices retain the operator’s currency and are normally per person sharing, unless stated otherwise. Check mandatory extras and refund terms; do not compare a reservation deposit with a full fare. Reviews concern existing ships, hotels or operators; none can review the unrun 2027 eclipse departure. Small samples, old reports, operator-hosted selections and uncertain hotel/vessel matches are identified.

Discovery also used the National Eclipse tour directory, cruise directory and the supplied Insight Cruises Luxor catalogue. Availability was checked on the actual departure or booking page; resellers of the same underlying offer are not counted as extra packages.
