# Eclipse 2027 package research and upkeep

Availability and review screening date: 10 October 2026 (UTC).

The deck compares packages that include viewing the 2 August 2027 total solar
eclipse. The five topics are classified by the eclipse viewing country/region,
not by embarkation airport, headquarters or every country visited. Oosterdam
belongs under Tunisia because its planned eclipse viewing is offshore Tunisia.
Lake Nasser itineraries can view the eclipse in Luxor or Abydos before sailing.

This is a package comparison across countries, rather than a locality guide.
Basic Info covers the common eclipse date, path, August heat and selection rules.
Population, founding history, neighborhoods and an unrelated festival/attraction
list would not describe this comparison and have not been added. Existing place
decks, including Egypt, remain separate.

## Evidence needed for inclusion

An exact 2027 departure must publish available places/rooms/cabins, explicitly
state available/limited availability, or expose a priced selectable allocation
in a public booking flow. A headline price, ordinary enquiry form, registration
link, selectable legacy cabin or directory price alone does not establish stock.
An explicit sold-out notice overrides a stale selector, catalogue or search
snippet. The page describes the evidence for each offer and distinguishes
provider-reported allocations from priced booking selections. No booking was
made, room held, personal information submitted or operator contacted.

For each included offer, confirm the departure, occupancy restriction, full fare,
mandatory extras and eclipse viewing plan. A whole-boat private charter is
labelled as such; an available slot is not necessarily suitable for a solo guest.
Keep original currencies. Different room and flight allocations can overlap.
Do not sum them or treat a deposit as the full price.

Available offers were found in all five topics, so no waitlist-only offer appears
on the main page. If a future user-requested search finds no available offer in a
country, include its waitlists and clearly label them.

## Retrieval and conflicts to check again

- Dabuka's entire rendered catalogue was expanded with Load More. Each included
  departure's remaining-place count and dated booking link was inspected. Some
  initial HTML responses were stale, empty or 404 while the browser exposed
  current dates and prices. Use a browser before concluding that a product has
  closed. Catalogue stock alone is insufficient: Nubian Journey advertised ten
  places but its product had no dates and a zero price, so it was excluded.
- Insight's st20, st32 and st33 cabin choices were advanced only to the public
  price calculation, without personal information. Compare overview, pricing
  terms and selector. st19 and st26 say sold out although their old selectors
  retain rooms. st33 prices four ocean-view categories while marking other
  classes sold out; the included E quote leaves facility fees and cruise-line
  gratuities additional. st32 has a US$35 per-person fee discrepancy and a
  departure-date discrepancy; both are shown on the page.
- META's ten-day Cleo, rail expeditions and other products have conflicts between
  detailed closures and catalogue availability. They remain excluded. MS Q and
  ten-day El Hanem explicitly report limited availability, with requests requiring
  later operator confirmation. META is distinct from Eclipse Travel Australia.
  The local eclipse times printed on those META offers conflict with astronomical
  predictions and have not been presented as correct viewing times.
- The MIT brochure supplied by the user returned 403. It remains an unverified
  lead with the original URL. Do not infer availability or eligibility from its
  existence, or substitute a different alumni departure without identifying it.
- Sirius's vacancy page now says waitlist only for every Egypt group despite
  its homepage suggesting remaining rooms. Classic Holidays' cabin allocation
  is sold despite retained prices. On the Go and TravelQuest dated pages also
  showed closures/waitlists. Old male/female room-share snippets are insufficient.
- Joe Capobianco's five-place headline and six-place widget conflict; both show
  space but the exact count needs confirmation. Some Dabuka offers conflict on
  itinerary dates or observing locality, and on older/newer deposit/cancellation
  terms. These limitations are attached to the affected offer or shared note.
- The directory contains many Egyptian variants and resellers. Additional leads
  are grouped by operator on the holding page, retaining their exact URLs and
  directory statuses. `discovery-only` means no confirmed allocation was
  established; it does not mean sold out. Many direct retrievals returned 503 or
  were blocked. These leads are useful for subsequent screening, and the main
  page is not a claim to list every package on the internet.

## Reviews

Match the exact business, ship or advertised hotel. Reviews of similarly named
operators were discarded. Every included offer has a review assessment, either
its own or an explicit link to the shared RJ/Dabuka assessment. The assessment
can describe an absence of dependable reviews; it must not manufacture a rating.
Hotel substitutions, new ship renovation, small samples, old reports,
operator-hosted selections and reviews of other countries are identified.
No review can yet concern the unrun 2027 departure. RJ's serious disputed
complaint is described as an allegation, with the operator response; aggregator
cross-posts must not be treated as independent corroboration.

## Maps

`assets/package-map.js` uses the shared StandardMap library for paired
OpenStreetMap/OpenTopoMap maps and clickable colored legends. The deck index
shows five country-region markers; Package options shows every named regional,
city and stay location mentioned in its text. Combined city/hotel markers are
explicitly approximate. They do not promise observing coordinates or draw an
eclipse path. The reject page has no map or navigation entry.

Geographic references include [Chaffar coordinates](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q2947561),
[Oued Laou](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oued_Laou),
[Al Tarfa's OpenStreetMap reference](https://mapcarta.com/W290086263) and
[Wadi Lahmy's OpenStreetMap reference](https://mapcarta.com/W677182473).
Each map popup links its orientation point to OpenStreetMap. Broad desert,
offshore and mooring-region points are deliberately labelled as regions.
The Wadi Lahmy/Hamata resort is within the boundaries in the
[NASA geographic path table](https://eclipse.gsfc.nasa.gov/SEpath/SEpath2001/SE2027Aug02Tpath.html);
its operator's exact advertised duration has not been recalculated here.
