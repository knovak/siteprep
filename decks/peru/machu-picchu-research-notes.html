How to read the availability checks

  • A main-list departure needs a dated public availability statement or selectable dated inventory from the operator or its booking platform. A brochure date, headline fare, enquiry or automated price request alone goes into holding. Record a minimum group size separately from available spaces.
  • Evidence for the four travelers is independent of general availability. A checkout total for four establishes a price for that group. A bedroom result requires the flow to offer two rooms together for the same four guests and departure. Record what happens after the room choice and whether the supplier still has to allocate rooms manually.
  • Four places in a shared cabin category or two private-room upgrades offered to solo guests do not establish a pair of doubles. Carry this distinction through homestays, trail lodges and overnight cruises. A classic Inca Trail trip with tents remains in holding under the current bedroom preference.
  • A dated tour can have space before it has the travelers’ named Machu Picchu tickets. Check which circuit, time, train and trail permit are included. The official circuit guide and the supplier’s terms answer different questions.
  • All current four-person costs use the quoted currency. Where the record multiplies a per-person rate by four, it labels that result as an estimate. Compare excluded domestic flights, booking and card fees, compulsory insurance, conditional discounts and the number of meals included.
