Jackson Hole

October 1–8 and December 23–30, 2026

Basic Info

  • Dates: Thursday, October 1–Thursday, October 8, 2026, and Wednesday, December 23–Wednesday, December 30, 2026.
  • Population: Jackson had 10,760 residents in the 2020 census; Jackson Hole is a valley rather than a census-defined metropolitan area.
  • Altitude and climate: Jackson sits at about 6,237 ft (1,901 m). The high-elevation continental climate has cold nights year-round. Early October often ranges from roughly 58°F/28°F (14°C/−2°C), with snow possible; late December is typically about 28°F/5°F (−2°C/−15°C), with persistent snow and shorter daylight. Pack traction, insulated layers, and winter-driving supplies.
  • Holidays: Indigenous Peoples’ Day/Columbus Day is Monday, October 12, within the week after the fall stay. Christmas Day is Friday, December 25, during the winter stay; many services close or reduce hours. New Year’s Day follows two days after the winter window.
  • History: Indigenous peoples used the valley for millennia. The Town of Jackson was named in 1894, platted in 1901, and incorporated in 1914; in 1920 it elected an all-woman town council. Read more on Wikipedia.

Attractions

  • National Museum of Wildlife Art: Wildlife-focused art museum overlooking the National Elk Refuge. Address: 2820 Rungius Road, Jackson, WY 83001. Hours: typically Tu–Su 10–5; verify seasonal and holiday closures. Official site | Google Maps.
  • Grand Teton National Park — Craig Thomas Discovery and Visitor Center: Gateway to mountain viewpoints and park information; winter access and services are limited. Address: 1 Teton Park Road, Moose, WY 83012. Hours: seasonal; check alerts and closures. NPS details | Google Maps.
  • National Elk Refuge: Protected winter habitat immediately north of town. Address: 675 E Broadway Avenue, Jackson, WY 83001. Hours: refuge open daylight hours; visitor and sleigh-ride schedules vary. USFWS | Google Maps.
  • Jackson Hole Mountain Resort: Major ski area in Teton Village; fall operations are limited and winter lifts depend on conditions. Address: 3395 Cody Lane, Teton Village, WY 83025. Hours: seasonal; check the daily operations report. Official site | Google Maps.

Neighborhoods

  • Town Square and Downtown Jackson: The most walkable visitor base for galleries, restaurants, shops, and bus connections.
  • East Jackson: Quieter residential area close to Snow King trails and the Elk Refuge edge.
  • West Jackson: Practical lodging, groceries, and local dining along the START bus network.
  • Teton Village: Resort village about 12 miles northwest of town; useful for skiing but not walkable to Jackson.

Ski and snowboard

  • Jackson Hole Mountain Resort: The valley’s large destination resort offers skiing and snowboarding from its Teton Village base, with an aerial tram, gondolas, chairlifts, a 4,139-foot vertical drop, and terrain best known for steep, advanced runs alongside beginner and intermediate areas. This is not Snow King Mountain, the smaller “ski hill” beside downtown Jackson; allow time to travel roughly 12 miles northwest from town to Teton Village.
  • Location: The supplied map pin marks Jackson Hole Resort Lodging in the resort’s Teton Village base area at 3200 McCollister Drive, Teton Village, WY 83025. Open the supplied location in Google Maps. From here, the lifts, rentals, lessons, restaurants, and other base-village services are close at hand.
  • Planning: Lift and terrain availability depend on snowfall and daily conditions. Hours are seasonal and vary by lift; check the official mountain report before leaving, and reserve lift tickets, rentals, and lessons ahead for the December holiday period.

Events and Seasonal Planning

  • October 1–8: This is shoulder season. Check the Jackson Hole Chamber calendar for newly announced arts, music, and community events. Grand Teton roads and visitor services begin seasonal closures; verify current park conditions before every outing.
  • December 23–30: Skiing and holiday programs are the focus, but operations remain weather-dependent. The National Elk Refuge’s horse-drawn sleigh rides generally operate during the snowy winter season; confirm the opening date, daily times, and reservations close to travel. Christmas Day closures are widespread.
  • Wildlife safety: Never approach or feed wildlife. Carry traction, monitor avalanche and road conditions, and allow extra winter travel time.
