Basic Info and trip brief

Search window: embark Thursday, April 15 through Sunday, June 20, 2027, inclusive. Trips may finish after June 20; the mainland portion of a combined package may begin earlier. Four adults travel as two couples, one couple in each of two private cabins. A 16-guest boat is preferred; 40 guests is the ceiling. There is no assumed budget or maximum cruise length. These are naturalist cruises with shore visits and snorkeling, not dedicated scuba trips.

Place and season: Galápagos Province had 28,583 residents in the 2022 census. Coastal embarkations are near sea level; the islands rise to 1,707 m. Ecuador annexed the islands in 1832, and the national park was established in 1959. See island essentials and attractions and history and geography. April–May is the warm/wet season: warm water, occasional heavy showers, and generally gentler seas, with cooling beginning in May. These are typical seasonal expectations, not a forecast; the strengthening 2026–27 El Niño may disrupt them. June normally has cooler water, a drier coast and windier crossings. Read El Niño effects and May–June tradeoffs and the climate guide.

Holiday planning: Ecuador observes Labor Day on Friday, April 30, 2027 (May 1 falls on Saturday), and the Battle of Pichincha on Monday, May 24. Allow room for mainland transport and hotel demand around these weekends. No date-specific island festival has been verified for inclusion. 2027 holiday calendar.
