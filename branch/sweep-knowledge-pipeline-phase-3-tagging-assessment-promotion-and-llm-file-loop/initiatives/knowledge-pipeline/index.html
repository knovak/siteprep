Purpose

2026-08-27

It should proceed in stages. Each stage begins with an AI process, and each may be supplemented by people taking action (often using skills).

  • Harvest: Collect from web searches, emails, or user ‘donations’, possibly collected via bookmarklet.
  • Tagging in multiple dimensions:. Examples are companies, geography, actor category (government versus private versus NGO versus individual), type of technology, social value area (peace, justice, well-being, environment.)
  • Selection and promotion:. Quality and importance are assessed.
  • Topic assignment:. An item can be directed to multiple topics. It is integrated into a sequence of mini narratives within a topic, to group related or redundant sources.
  • Topic integration. Every topic has zero to many standing documents which provide a curated set of conclusions or positions regarding the topic. The growing set of narratives from the topic assignment stage are compared against the standing documents to identify the volume and urgency of the new items that have arrived. It is then up to a human to author to update the standing documents, and afterwards to migrate the mini narratives into a reference archive.

While there is some summarization and combination happening from one stage to another, all original references are retained from beginning to end. Backward links from later stages to prior stages are also maintained for auditing and quality review.
