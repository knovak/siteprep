Oran, Algeria

Friday, July 30–Thursday, August 5, 2027

Mediterranean heritage, modern art, and roughly five minutes of totality.

Basic Info

  • Dates: Friday, July 30–Thursday, August 5, 2027; eclipse day is Monday, August 2.
  • Population: About 803,000 in the city proper (2008 census figure) and roughly 1.5–1.6 million in the wider metropolitan area (later estimates). The boundaries and dates differ; these are orientation figures. City profile.
  • Altitude and climate: The city rises from the Mediterranean waterfront into hills; the Santa Cruz fort stands roughly 400 m above the bay. Summers are hot and very dry, with late-July/early-August afternoons around 30–32°C (86–90°F) and nights around 20–22°C (68–72°F). Coastal haze or low cloud remains possible. These are seasonal expectations, not a 2027 forecast. Climate averages.
  • Holidays: No regular national holiday is listed during July 23–August 12, covering the stay and one week on either side. The Prophet’s Birthday is tentatively August 15, outside that window; lunar dates and special closures should be rechecked. 2027 holiday calendar.
  • Crime: Pickpocketing, bag snatching, and theft from cars are visitor risks, especially in larger cities and after dark. Keep valuables out of sight and use arranged transport at night. Current travel advice.
  • History: Founded around 902–903 CE, Oran became a Mediterranean trading port, passed through Spanish and Ottoman rule, and developed extensively under French rule before Algerian independence in 1962. It is also a major center of raï music. History on Wikipedia.

Solar Eclipse — August 2, 2027

Oran is a total-eclipse base. A site with clear sky and an unobstructed morning view matters more than choosing the highest viewpoint. Confirm access, shade, drinking water, toilets, and return transport; Santa Cruz’s regular visitor access does not guarantee an eclipse booking. See Oran’s eclipse timings.

Eye safety: Use ISO 12312-2 eclipse glasses whenever any bright part of the Sun is visible. Remove them only during totality, at a location actually inside the totality path. Cameras, binoculars, and telescopes need suitable filters over the front of the optics. NASA viewing guidance.

Attractions

  • Santa Cruz fort and chapel {499}: Spanish fortifications and a white hillside sanctuary above the bay. Arrange a car for the steep approach; check both entrances separately. An attractive panorama does not establish eclipse-day access. Address: Route de Santa Cruz, Mount Murdjadjo, Oran, Algeria; طريق سانتا كروز، جبل مرجاجو، وهران، الجزائر. Hours: Daytime visits; confirm gate and chapel hours locally. Visitor information | Google Maps.
  • Bey’s Palace {995}: An Ottoman-era palace and courtyards in the old city, a useful starting point for understanding Oran’s layers of history. Address: Rue Meftah Kouider, Sidi El Houari, Oran, Algeria; شارع مفتاح قويدر، سيدي الهواري، وهران، الجزائر. Hours: Access and guided visits vary; confirm before going. Palace background | Google Maps.
  • MAMO — Museum of Modern and Contemporary Art {367}: Algerian modern and contemporary art in the former Galeries de France department store, converted into a museum in 2017. Exhibitions and accessible floors can change. Address: 5–7 Rue Larbi Ben M’Hidi, Oran 31000, Algeria; ٥–٧ شارع العربي بن مهيدي، وهران ٣١٠٠٠، الجزائر. Hours: Confirm current exhibition hours with the museum. Museum website | Google Maps.
  • Ahmed Zabana National Museum {857}: Archaeology, natural history, ethnography, and fine art offer a broad introduction to western Algeria. Address: 19 Avenue Ahmed Zabana, Oran 31000, Algeria; ١٩ شارع أحمد زبانة، وهران ٣١٠٠٠، الجزائر. Hours: Confirm current opening days and hours locally. Visitor details | Google Maps.
  • Place du 1er Novembre and regional theatre {141}: The central square, city hall’s lion statues, and the ornate Abdelkader Alloula theatre make an easy downtown walk. Theatre admission requires a scheduled performance or arranged visit. Address: Place du 1er Novembre 1954, Oran, Algeria; ساحة أول نوفمبر ١٩٥٤، وهران، الجزائر. Hours: Square outdoors; theatre by published program. Theatre program | Google Maps.

Neighborhoods

  • Downtown and Place du 1er Novembre: The central visitor area around the square, theatre, cafés, and civic buildings; walkable in short stretches, with shade breaks.
  • Sidi El Houari: The historic quarter around the Bey’s Palace, below the Santa Cruz slopes. Expect uneven streets and steep sections; a local guide helps with access and context.
  • Medina Jedida: A busy market district south of downtown, useful for everyday shopping and street life; the map marks Place Tahtaha as its reference point.
  • Akid Lotfi: A newer eastern district with restaurants and modern accommodation; more dispersed and better suited to short taxi rides than a continuous sightseeing walk.

Events

  • Total solar eclipse — Monday, August 2 {736}: Oran is inside totality: maximum is around 9:53am local time (UTC+1), with about 5 minutes 9 seconds of totality at the city reference point. The partial phases run roughly 8:44–11:09am. Scout an open eastern/southeastern view and arrange transport in advance. The square is a map reference, not an announced observing venue. Address: Place du 1er Novembre 1954, Oran, Algeria; ساحة أول نوفمبر ١٩٥٤، وهران، الجزائر. Hours: Morning; exact contacts depend on the observing coordinates.
