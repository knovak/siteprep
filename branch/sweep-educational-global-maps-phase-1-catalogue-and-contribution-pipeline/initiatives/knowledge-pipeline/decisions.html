2026-08-31 — May Knowledge Pipeline deploy public Sites?

Yes. Permission is granted for Knowledge Pipeline deployments as public Sites. This is standing permission, recorded so future phases do not stop to ask the same access question again.

What this settles, and what it does not

  • Test and production Sites for this initiative may be created or updated with public Site access once the applicable build, test, and lifecycle gates pass.
  • Public Site access does not make knowledge public: data, API, object, export, and administration routes remain authenticated and allowlist-gated.
  • This does not authorize creating a heartbeat automation, bypassing a release gate, or choosing the initiative's eventual distribution model.
