Aspen, Colorado

December 20–31, 2026

Basic Info

  • Dates: Sunday, December 20–Thursday, December 31, 2026.
  • Population: Aspen had 7,004 residents in the 2020 census. The Aspen micropolitan area, coterminous with Pitkin County, had 17,358 residents.
  • Altitude and climate: Downtown is about 7,908 ft (2,410 m) above sea level. Aspen has a high-elevation continental climate with snowy winters, strong sun, and large day–night temperature swings. Late December typically brings daytime highs around 30°F (−1°C) and lows near 10°F (−12°C); heavier snow and colder spells are possible. Acclimatize, hydrate, use sun protection, and carry traction and warm layers.
  • Holidays: Christmas Day is Friday, December 25, during the stay; expect closures and reduced hours. Kwanzaa runs December 26–January 1, and New Year’s Day is Friday, January 1, immediately after the stay.
  • Safety: Crime is a low concern for visitors. Theft can occur, so lock vehicles and lodging, secure skis and bicycles, and use normal precautions around nightlife.
  • History: Ute people lived in and traveled through the Roaring Fork Valley long before prospectors founded a mining camp here in 1879. Renamed Aspen in 1880 and incorporated in 1881, the town boomed with silver, declined after 1893, and was remade as a ski and cultural destination in the mid-20th century. Read more on Wikipedia.

Attractions

  • Aspen Art Museum: A free, non-collecting contemporary art museum in a Shigeru Ban-designed building. Address: 637 E Hyman Avenue, Aspen, CO 81611. Hours: typically Tu–Su 10–6; verify holiday hours. Official site | Google Maps.
  • Aspen Mountain and Silver Queen Gondola: The downtown ski mountain, with gondola access, expert-oriented terrain, and summit views. Address: 601 E Dean Street, Aspen, CO 81611. Hours: lift operations are seasonal and weather-dependent; consult the daily report. Official mountain guide | Google Maps.
  • John Denver Sanctuary: A landscaped riverside garden with engraved boulders and mountain views; paths may be snowy or icy. Address: 470 Rio Grande Place, Aspen, CO 81611. Hours: daily, dawn–dusk. City details | Google Maps.
  • Maroon Bells Scenic Area: Iconic twin peaks in the White River National Forest. In winter, Maroon Creek Road is closed to motor vehicles beyond T-Lazy-7 Ranch; the snow-covered route is for prepared Nordic skiers, snowshoers, and snowmobiles rather than a casual road visit. Winter access point: T-Lazy-7 Ranch, 3129 Maroon Creek Road, Aspen, CO 81611. Hours: access is weather- and conditions-dependent. Forest Service information | Google Maps.
  • Wheeler Opera House: Aspen’s 1889 performance hall presents concerts, films, comedy, and community programs. Address: 320 E Hyman Avenue, Aspen, CO 81611. Hours: box office and performance hours vary. Official calendar | Google Maps.

Neighborhoods

  • Downtown Core: The most walkable visitor base, centered on the pedestrian malls around Hyman and Cooper Avenues, with restaurants, galleries, shops, the art museum, and the gondola.
  • West End: A quiet, walkable historic district of Victorian homes and cultural institutions northwest of downtown.
  • East Aspen: A residential area following the Roaring Fork River east of the core, served by local trails and buses.
  • Smuggler: A residential hillside area northeast of downtown, best known to visitors for the popular Smuggler Mountain trail.
  • Aspen Highlands: A compact ski-base village about 3 miles from downtown; use the free RFTA bus rather than relying on parking.
