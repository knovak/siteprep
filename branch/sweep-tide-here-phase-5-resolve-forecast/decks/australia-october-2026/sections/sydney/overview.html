Sydney, New South Wales

Wednesday, October 14–Saturday, October 17, 2026

Basic Info

  • Dates: Wednesday, October 14 through Saturday, October 17, 2026.
  • Population: The City of Sydney local government area had 211,632 residents in the 2021 census; Greater Sydney had about 5.23 million.
  • Altitude and climate: Central Sydney is near sea level, with its highest natural point around 42 m. Mid-October is mild spring weather, commonly near 22°C by day and 14°C at night, with showers, wind, high UV, and warmer spells possible.
  • Holidays: No New South Wales public holiday falls during the stay or adjacent week.
  • History: Sydney stands on the Country of the Gadigal and neighboring peoples, whose continuing cultures reach back tens of thousands of years. The British colony at Sydney Cove was founded on January 26, 1788. Read more on Wikipedia.

Attractions

  • Sydney Opera House {418}: Jørn Utzon’s UNESCO-listed performing-arts landmark offers tours and harbor views as well as a busy performance program. Address: Bennelong Point, Sydney NSW 2000, Australia. Hours: Daily; tour and performance times vary. Official information | Google Maps.
  • Art Gallery of New South Wales {763}: The state gallery combines Australian, Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander, Asian, European, and contemporary art across two buildings. Address: Art Gallery Road, Sydney NSW 2000, Australia. Hours: Daily 10–5; W to 10. Official information | Google Maps.
  • Museum of Contemporary Art Australia {251}: Contemporary Australian and international art in a former maritime-services building at Circular Quay. Address: 140 George Street, The Rocks NSW 2000, Australia. Hours: Tu–Su 10–5. Official information | Google Maps.
  • Sydney Harbour National Park {906}: Harbour islands, headlands, bushland, beaches, and historic sites protect natural and cultural heritage around the city. Address: Bradleys Head Road, Mosman NSW 2088, Australia. Hours: Daylight access; individual sites vary. Official information | Google Maps.

Neighborhoods

  • The Rocks and Circular Quay: Historic lanes, harbor ferries, museums, and the Opera House make this the most visitor-focused waterfront district.
  • Surry Hills: A walkable inner-city area known for cafes, restaurants, design, and small galleries; the map pin marks Shannon Reserve.
  • Newtown: King Street is a lively, walkable spine of independent shops, restaurants, street art, and music venues.
  • Darlinghurst and Paddington: Terraces, Oxford Street culture, galleries, and Saturday’s Paddington Markets make these adjoining districts rewarding on foot.

Events

  • The Rocks Markets, October 16–17 {584}: A Friday food market followed by a weekend market of local design, art, crafts, and food in historic laneways. Address: George and Playfair Streets, The Rocks NSW 2000, Australia. Hours: F 10–3, Sa 10–5. Official information | Google Maps.
  • Paddington Markets, October 17 {329}: Long-running Saturday community market featuring Australian-made fashion, art, jewelry, and food. Address: 395 Oxford Street, Paddington NSW 2021, Australia. Hours: Sa 10–4. Official information | Google Maps.
