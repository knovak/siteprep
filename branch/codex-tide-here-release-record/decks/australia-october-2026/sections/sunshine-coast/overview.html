Sunshine Coast, Queensland

Friday, October 16–Saturday, November 21, 2026

Basic Info

  • Dates: Friday, October 16 through Saturday, November 21, 2026.
  • Population: The Sunshine Coast Region local government area had 342,541 residents in the 2021 census; the wider urban area is part of southeast Queensland rather than a separate official metropolitan statistical area.
  • Altitude and climate: Beaches are at sea level while the hinterland rises above 400 m. Late spring is warm and increasingly humid, typically around 25–28°C by day and 16–20°C at night, with strong UV, showers, thunderstorms, and warm ocean water.
  • Holidays: No Queensland public holiday falls during the stay. Halloween (October 31) is not a public holiday; Melbourne Cup Day (November 3) is widely observed socially but is not a Queensland public holiday.
  • History: The region is the Country of the Kabi Kabi and Jinibara peoples. European pastoral settlement expanded from the 1860s; the modern Sunshine Coast Region was formed in 2008. Read more on Wikipedia.

Attractions

  • Noosa National Park {418}: Coastal tracks pass rainforest, beaches, rocky headlands, and wildlife habitat immediately beside Noosa Heads. Address: Park Road, Noosa Heads QLD 4567, Australia. Hours: Open 24h; walk in daylight. Official information | Google Maps.
  • Caloundra Regional Gallery {763}: The regional public gallery presents contemporary exhibitions with a strong focus on Sunshine Coast artists. Address: 22 Omrah Avenue, Caloundra QLD 4551, Australia. Hours: Tu–F 10–4, Sa–Su 10–2. Official information | Google Maps.
  • Maleny Botanic Gardens & Bird World {251}: Privately operated hillside gardens and aviaries with views toward the Glass House Mountains. Address: 233 Maleny Stanley River Road, Maleny QLD 4552, Australia. Hours: Daily 9–4:30. Official information | Google Maps.
  • Glass House Mountains National Park {906}: A cluster of dramatic volcanic plugs with lookouts, walking tracks, and deep cultural significance to the Kabi Kabi and Jinibara peoples. Address: Glass House–Woodford Road, Glass House Mountains QLD 4518, Australia. Hours: Open 24h; walk in daylight. Official information | Google Maps.

Events

  • Eumundi Markets, Wednesdays and Saturdays {584}: Major recurring arts-and-crafts market with locally made goods, food, and live entertainment; multiple market days fall within the stay. Address: 80 Memorial Drive, Eumundi QLD 4562, Australia. Hours: W 8–2, Sa 7–2. Official information | Google Maps.
  • Caloundra Street Fair, Sundays {329}: Weekly street market with food, fashion, gifts, live music, and community stalls; every Sunday in the date range is eligible. Address: Bulcock Street, Caloundra QLD 4551, Australia. Hours: Su 8–1. Official information | Google Maps.
  • Noosa Farmers Market, October 18 and 25; November 1, 8, and 15 {742}: A Sunday growers’ market under the gum trees, focused on locally produced seasonal fruit, vegetables, seafood, meat, bread, preserves, and prepared food. Address: 155 Weyba Road, Noosaville QLD 4566, Australia. Hours: Su 7–12. Official information | Google Maps.
