Stradbroke Island

Monday, October 26–Saturday, October 31, 2026

Basic Info

  • Place: Minjerribah (North Stradbroke Island), the second-largest sand island in the world, in Moreton Bay east of Brisbane.
  • Population: about 2,000 permanent residents across Dunwich, Amity Point, and Point Lookout; the island is not a metropolitan area.
  • Altitude and terrain: sea level at the beaches, rising to roughly 239 m at the island’s highest dunes; expect sand tracks, headlands, wetlands, and freshwater lakes.
  • Climate: humid subtropical and moderated by the sea. Late October is normally warm: typical daytime highs near 25°C and overnight lows near 17°C, with sunshine, showers, strong UV, and changeable coastal winds all possible.
  • Dates and daylight: Monday, October 26 through Saturday, October 31, 2026. Pack sun protection, a light rain layer, insect repellent, and a warmer layer for breezy evenings.
  • Holidays: no regular Queensland public holiday falls during the stay or in the preceding/following week. Halloween is Saturday, October 31, but it is not a public holiday.
  • History: Minjerribah is Quandamooka Country, cared for by its Traditional Owners for tens of thousands of years. James Cook recorded European sighting in 1770; a British settlement was founded at Dunwich in 1827. Sand mining shaped the modern economy before ending in 2019. Read more on Wikipedia.

Attractions

  • North Gorge Walk: A short clifftop boardwalk with dramatic ocean views and opportunities to spot dolphins, turtles, manta rays, and migrating whales. Address: Gorge Walk entrance, Mooloomba Road, Point Lookout QLD 4183. Hours: Open 24h; visit in daylight and keep clear of cliff edges. Google Maps.
  • Cylinder Beach: Popular, comparatively sheltered swimming beach below Point Lookout; swim only between the flags when lifesavers are present. Address: Cylinder Beach Foreshore, Point Lookout QLD 4183. Hours: Open 24h; patrol hours vary. Google Maps.
  • Bummiera (Brown Lake): A culturally significant perched lake whose tea-colored water is stained by surrounding vegetation; treat the area respectfully and follow signed access guidance. Address: Brown Lake Road, Dunwich QLD 4183. Hours: Daylight access. Google Maps.
  • Naree Budjong Djara National Park: Quandamooka Country protected for freshwater lakes, wetlands, heath, forest, and wildlife. Check Queensland Parks alerts and access rules before setting out; some routes require a high-clearance 4WD permit. Address: Blue Lake (Karboora) track access, Alfred Martin Way, North Stradbroke Island QLD 4183. Hours: Daylight visits recommended. Queensland Parks | Google Maps.
  • North Stradbroke Island Historical Museum: Community museum interpreting Quandamooka, mission, maritime, mining, and island social history. Address: 15–17 Welsby Street, Dunwich QLD 4183. Hours: Tu–Sa 10–2; confirm before travel. Museum website | Google Maps.
