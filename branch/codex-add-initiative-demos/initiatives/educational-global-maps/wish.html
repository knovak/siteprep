Wish

2026-08-26

Educational global maps. Provide an interactive environment with many options. These will combine the following.

  • Geographic time series data sets. These may come from our world in data or from the sources for the SOS (science on a sphere) displays.  Anticipate perhaps hundreds in a catalogue.  Accommodate region, country, district, city level resolution.
  • Visualizations. These may map a single data set, or they may combine data sets, for example, with layering or with flows on top of static data.
  • Projections. The visualization could be provided over a conventional map, a Dymaxion map, or possibly a map distorted to be proportional to population.
  • Display. Utilize resolution up to that of a large 4K conventional display. Ideally use AirPlay other wireless connection. Allow the sliders and pan and zoom controls to be off screen, such as on a handheld or laptop device.  Be open to projection onto spherical displays.

Besides the general public, the expected users of this would be high school and undergraduate students.  The plan would be to define interfaces and procedures so that new data sets could be added by others over time.
