Brisbane, Queensland

Friday, October 16–Saturday, November 21, 2026

Basic Info

  • Dates: Friday, October 16 through Saturday, November 21, 2026.
  • Population: Brisbane City local government area had about 1.24 million residents in the 2021 census; Greater Brisbane had about 2.57 million.
  • Altitude and climate: The riverfront is near sea level and the city rises to 287 m at Mount Coot-tha. Late spring is warm and increasingly humid, commonly 26–29°C by day and 17–20°C at night, with high UV and building thunderstorm risk.
  • Holidays: No Queensland public holiday falls during the stay. Halloween (October 31) is not a public holiday; Melbourne Cup Day (November 3) is socially prominent but not a Queensland public holiday.
  • History: Brisbane occupies the Country of the Turrbal and Jagera peoples. The Moreton Bay penal settlement moved to the Brisbane River in 1825; free settlement opened in 1842 and Brisbane became a municipality in 1859. Read more on Wikipedia.

Attractions

  • Queensland Art Gallery & Gallery of Modern Art {418}: QAGOMA’s two riverfront buildings hold Queensland’s leading collections of Australian, Indigenous, Asian, Pacific, and international art. Address: Stanley Place, South Brisbane QLD 4101, Australia. Hours: Daily 10–5. Official information | Google Maps.
  • Museum of Brisbane {763}: City Hall museum exploring Brisbane’s people, art, design, and civic history. Address: Level 3, Brisbane City Hall, 64 Adelaide Street, Brisbane City QLD 4000, Australia. Hours: Daily 10–5. Official information | Google Maps.
  • Brisbane Botanic Gardens Mount Coot-tha {251}: Subtropical and themed gardens spread below Brisbane’s highest prominent lookout. Address: 152 Mount Coot-tha Road, Mount Coot-tha QLD 4066, Australia. Hours: Daily 8–6. Official information | Google Maps.
  • D’Aguilar National Park {906}: A nearby national park protecting eucalypt forest, rainforest, walking trails, lookouts, and wildlife habitat. Address: Walkabout Creek Discovery Centre, 60 Mount Nebo Road, The Gap QLD 4061, Australia. Hours: Daylight access; individual areas vary. Official information | Google Maps.

Neighborhoods

  • Brisbane City and Howard Smith Wharves: Walkable riverfront, civic sights, shopping arcades, and dining; the map pin marks Brisbane City Hall.
  • South Bank and West End: Museums, parklands, river paths, markets, and diverse dining make this the strongest visitor precinct on foot.
  • Fortitude Valley and New Farm: Live music, nightlife, design shops, Chinatown, and New Farm Park connect two lively inner-city districts.
  • Paddington: Hilly streets of Queenslander houses, antiques, cafes, and small shops along Given and Latrobe terraces.
