Boulder, Colorado

Friday, September 18–Tuesday, September 22, 2026

Basic Info

  • Dates: Friday, September 18 through Tuesday, September 22, 2026.
  • Population: Boulder counted 108,250 residents in the 2020 census; Boulder County, the Boulder metropolitan statistical area, counted 330,758.
  • Altitude and climate: Downtown is about 5,430 ft (1,655 m). The semi-arid foothills climate usually brings sunny September afternoons near 77°F (25°C), nights near 48°F (9°C), low humidity, and occasional thunderstorms; pack layers and sun protection.
  • Holidays: No regular U.S. federal or Colorado state holiday falls during the stay or adjacent week. The autumnal equinox is Tuesday, September 22.
  • History: On the traditional lands of Indigenous peoples including the Arapaho, Boulder City was founded in 1859 during the Colorado gold rush and incorporated in 1871. Read more on Wikipedia.
Hotel Boulderado: A landmark 1909 downtown hotel one block from Pearl Street, with a stained-glass lobby and historic character. Address: 2115 13th Street, Boulder, CO 80302. Times: check-in 4pm; check-out 11am. Official hotel site | Google Maps.

Attractions

  • Museum of Boulder {482}: A contemporary local-history museum in the landmark Masonic Lodge, with exhibits on Boulder people and innovation. Address: 2205 Broadway, Boulder, CO 80302. Hours: Tu–Su 10–5. Official information | Google Maps.
  • Boulder Museum of Contemporary Art {735}: A non-collecting contemporary-art museum presenting changing exhibitions in a 1906 warehouse. Address: 1750 13th Street, Boulder, CO 80302. Hours: Tu–Su 11–5. Official information | Google Maps.
  • Chautauqua Park {196}: The historic Chautauqua campus is a gateway to Flatirons trails and includes landmark cottages and an auditorium. Address: 900 Baseline Road, Boulder, CO 80302. Hours: Park daily 5–11. Official information | Google Maps.
  • Eldorado Canyon State Park {864}: A nearby state park known for towering sandstone cliffs, climbing, hiking, and South Boulder Creek. Address: 9 Kneale Road, Eldorado Springs, CO 80025. Hours: Daily, seasonal hours; timed vehicle entry may apply. Official information | Google Maps.
  • Fall colors {672}: Boulder’s cottonwoods, sumacs, maples, and oaks brighten trails and town streets in fall; the city recommends Coot Lake, Gregory Canyon, South Mesa, Panorama Point, Chautauqua, Bobolink Trail, Sawhill Ponds, Mapleton Avenue, and Central Park, with peak color generally in early October. Check real-time parking and weather before leaving. Reference location: Chautauqua Park, 900 Baseline Road, Boulder, CO 80302. Hours: Park daily 5–11. City fall-color guide | Trailhead cameras | Google Maps.

Neighborhoods

  • Pearl Street and Downtown: The pedestrian mall is the visitor-oriented heart of town; the map pin marks Pearl Street Mall at 1303 Pearl Street.
  • University Hill: A lively student district west of the University of Colorado; the map pin marks The Hill at 13th Street and Pennsylvania Avenue.
  • NoBo Arts District: Studios, galleries, and creative businesses line north Broadway; the map pin marks the NoBo Art Center, 4929 Broadway.
