Stradbroke Island

Monday, October 26–Saturday, October 31, 2026

Basic Info

  • Place: Minjerribah (North Stradbroke Island), the second-largest sand island in the world, in Moreton Bay east of Brisbane.
  • Population: about 2,000 permanent residents across Dunwich, Amity Point, and Point Lookout; the island is not a metropolitan area.
  • Altitude and terrain: sea level at the beaches, rising to roughly 239 m at the island’s highest dunes; expect sand tracks, headlands, wetlands, and freshwater lakes.
  • Climate: humid subtropical and moderated by the sea. Late October is normally warm: typical daytime highs near 25°C and overnight lows near 17°C, with sunshine, showers, strong UV, and changeable coastal winds all possible.
  • Dates and daylight: Monday, October 26 through Saturday, October 31, 2026. Pack sun protection, a light rain layer, insect repellent, and a warmer layer for breezy evenings.
  • Holidays: no regular Queensland public holiday falls during the stay or in the preceding/following week. Halloween is Saturday, October 31, but it is not a public holiday.
  • Safety: Crime is a low concern for visitors. Lock vehicles and accommodation, keep valuables out of sight, and use normal precautions around beaches and isolated areas.
  • History: Minjerribah is Quandamooka Country, cared for by its Traditional Owners for tens of thousands of years. James Cook recorded European sighting in 1770; a British settlement was founded at Dunwich in 1827. Sand mining shaped the modern economy before ending in 2019. Read more on Wikipedia.

3. Cleveland station to the ferry

Stradbroke Flyer courtesy bus

  • Free for Flyer passengers; travels between the station and Flyer terminal.
  • The operator’s posted station pickup times are: 6:35, 7:35, 8:40, 9:55, 11:10 am; 12:25, 1:40, 3:10, 4:35, 5:50, 6:55 pm.
  • These are generally 10–20 minutes before ferry departure, not uniformly 10 minutes. Be at the pickup point early and allow time to get off the train.
  • No station pickup is listed for the 4:45 am, 5:45 am or 8:15 pm departures from Cleveland.
  • The ferry timetable also marks no Flyer courtesy bus for the island departures at 5:15 am, 7:45 pm and 8:45 pm. Confirm return connections with the operator.
  • Mainland shuttle and island route 880 are separate services.

Source: Flyer timetable, courtesy-bus schedule and fares. Phone: 07 3821 3821.

Bus 258 for SeaLink

  • Travels from Cleveland station to Toondah Harbour for SeaLink.
  • Allow approximately 10 minutes riding time, plus waiting; exact departures should be checked for your date.
  • Standard Translink fare: A$0.50 per journey. The private ferry is not included.
  • SeaLink recommends arrival at its terminal 30 minutes before departure. Plan the train/bus connection accordingly.

6. Island bus to George Nothling Drive

  • Board route 880 toward Point Lookout at either island ferry terminal. Do not take route 881 to Amity Point.
  • Get off at Dickson Way at George Nothling Drive; ask the driver to identify your stop.
  • Allow roughly 30–40 minutes from the ferry terminal to this area, depending on the terminal and service.
  • Walk along George Nothling Drive to No. 15. The exact walking distance, gradient and footpath conditions have not been verified; check with your host if managing heavy luggage.
  • Google Maps walking directions: bus-stop area to the house.

The bus serves both ferry terminals seven days a week, but not every early/late ferry has a connection. The timetable also includes extra weekend, public-holiday and school-holiday services. It identifies Christmas Day as an exception requiring confirmation. Be at your return bus stop at least five minutes early.

8. Practical choice

  • Lowest-cost arrival: either ferry plus route 880; the calculated adult one-way cost from Cleveland station is the same at A$14.50.
  • Simplest mainland transfer: Flyer has its own free station shuttle, but use the actual pickup times above.
  • Easiest with luggage: prearrange an island taxi or StradEasy pickup from your chosen ferry to the house.
  • Return travel: choose the ferry first, then work backward through the island bus or booked driver and the Cleveland station connection. Do not assume the last ferry has a connecting bus.

Attractions

  • North Gorge Walk: A short clifftop boardwalk with dramatic ocean views and opportunities to spot dolphins, turtles, manta rays, and migrating whales. Address: Gorge Walk entrance, Mooloomba Road, Point Lookout QLD 4183. Hours: Open 24h; visit in daylight and keep clear of cliff edges. Google Maps.
  • Cylinder Beach: Popular, comparatively sheltered swimming beach below Point Lookout; swim only between the flags when lifesavers are present. Address: Cylinder Beach Foreshore, Point Lookout QLD 4183. Hours: Open 24h; patrol hours vary. Google Maps.
  • Bummiera (Brown Lake): A culturally significant perched lake whose tea-colored water is stained by surrounding vegetation; treat the area respectfully and follow signed access guidance. Address: Brown Lake Road, Dunwich QLD 4183. Hours: Daylight access. Google Maps.
  • Naree Budjong Djara National Park: Quandamooka Country protected for freshwater lakes, wetlands, heath, forest, and wildlife. Check Queensland Parks alerts and access rules before setting out; some routes require a high-clearance 4WD permit. Address: Blue Lake (Karboora) track access, Alfred Martin Way, North Stradbroke Island QLD 4183. Hours: Daylight visits recommended. Queensland Parks | Google Maps.
  • North Stradbroke Island Historical Museum: Community museum interpreting Quandamooka, mission, maritime, mining, and island social history. Address: 15–17 Welsby Street, Dunwich QLD 4183. Hours: Tu–Sa 10–2; confirm before travel. Museum website | Google Maps.
