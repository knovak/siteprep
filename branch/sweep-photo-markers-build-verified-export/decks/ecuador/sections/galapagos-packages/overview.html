Galápagos Islands · Research and upkeep notes

Basic Info and trip brief

Search window: embark Thursday, April 15 through Monday, May 31, 2027, inclusive. Trips may finish in June. Four adults travel as two couples, one couple in each of two private cabins. A 16-guest boat is preferred; 40 guests is the ceiling. There is no assumed budget or maximum cruise length. These are naturalist cruises with shore visits and snorkeling, not dedicated scuba trips.

Place and season: Galápagos Province had 28,583 residents in the 2022 census. Coastal embarkations are near sea level; the islands rise to 1,707 m. Ecuador annexed the islands in 1832, and the national park was established in 1959. See island essentials and attractions and history and geography. April–May is the warm/wet season: warm water, occasional heavy showers, and generally gentler seas, with cooling beginning in May. These are seasonal expectations, not a forecast. Climate guide.

Holiday planning: Ecuador observes Labor Day on Friday, April 30, 2027 (May 1 falls on Saturday), and the Battle of Pichincha on Monday, May 24. Allow room for mainland transport and hotel demand around these weekends. No date-specific island festival has been verified for inclusion. 2027 holiday calendar.

Best-established room fit: Grace, May 25–June 1 {461} exposed two queen-bed cabins together for four adults at US$45,600 cruise-only, with park and transit fees included. Archipel I, April 19–22 {257} exposed two private rooms at US$9,308 cruise-only, one twin and one queen; adding park/transit fees brings the known subtotal to US$10,188. Isabela II, May 6–10 {872} also priced two private twin-bed rooms together at US$17,844 cruise-only, US$18,724 with park/TCT before fuel/carbon fees and other extras. These are public pricing results, subject to supplier confirmation. No booking or hold was made.

How to read availability: every main-list departure has a dated public stock signal; “AVAILABLE” or four remaining spaces does not establish two private cabins. The separate party column records what was actually exposed. Unless explicitly stated, two cabins remain unconfirmed. Checked October 20, 2026 (UTC); inventory and fares can change.

Price basis: USD, per adult sharing, with a four-person subtotal in each entry. Known mandatory charges are included in the sortable subtotal; air, mainland hotels, insurance, tips and unpriced supplements are identified separately. Unless already included, budget US$200 national-park entry plus US$20 transit card per foreign adult: US$880 for four. These are current published fees, not a promise of unchanged 2027 rates. Official park fee. Different lengths and inclusions make a cheaper total an imperfect value comparison.

Routes: Grace's eight-day voyage gives substantially more island time than a four-day Archipel sailing. For western Isabela/Fernandina, inspect Isabela II's Islands of Fire route and Evolution’s In the Steps of Pirates & Darwin route, plus the Integrity, Natural Paradise and Ecoventura leads in research holding. For April–May albatross viewing, choose a route explicitly including Española. Wildlife sightings and landing sites are never guaranteed.
