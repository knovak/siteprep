2026-11-11 — Beta: settings export, import and presets

At the user's request, added a "Presets & settings" panel to the beta only. It exports every setting as a YAML text file, imports such a file, and offers three presets: New York City 30 minutes after sunset on October 20, Taos 30 minutes before sunrise on March 15, and Alice Springs 60 minutes after sunset on June 20. Each preset is one file in beta/presets/; adding or deleting a file adds or removes a preset.

beta/src/settings.js is new and beta/src/ui.js gained the panel's code. beta/src/model.js and beta/src/render.js are unchanged, and so are lib/, work/ and demos/SBDC Night Sky/. beta/README.md describes the file format and how to add a preset.
