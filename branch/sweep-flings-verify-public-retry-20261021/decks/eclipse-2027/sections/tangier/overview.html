Tangier and nearby region, Morocco

Friday, July 30–Thursday, August 5, 2027

A total-eclipse base with the medina, modern art, Atlantic headlands, and northern Moroccan excursions.

Basic Info

  • Dates: Friday, July 30–Thursday, August 5, 2027; eclipse day is Monday, August 2.
  • Population: The Tangier commune had 1,275,428 residents in the 2024 census. A broader metropolitan/urban estimate is about 1.4 million in the mid-2020s; definitions differ. City / census background | Metropolitan estimates.
  • Altitude and climate: Tangier climbs from sea level into hills, with the city’s highest ground around 230 m. Late July and early August are warm, generally dry, and breezy: typical highs 29–30°C (84–86°F), lows around 20–21°C (68–70°F). The Atlantic and strait can bring haze, low cloud, or strong wind despite the dry season. Climate averages.
  • Holidays: Throne Day is Friday, July 30, the arrival day and a national public holiday. Expect some offices to close and holiday traffic; local celebrations depend on the announced city program. No other regular holiday is listed through August 12, the end of the following-week check. Religious dates may shift. 2027 holiday calendar.
  • Crime: Pickpocketing, bag snatching, and tourist scams are concerns in crowded areas. Keep bags secure and arrange reliable transport after dark. Current travel advice.
  • History: A settlement traditionally dated to around 1000 BCE became Phoenician and Roman Tingis, later passed through Portuguese and English rule, and was an international zone in the 20th century before returning to Moroccan control in 1956. Its port and artistic history reflect these connections. History on Wikipedia.

Solar Eclipse — August 2, 2027

Tangier is inside totality. Select a site with an open east/southeast view; scout haze, terrain, shade, toilets, and transport access. The coastal attractions below are places to explore, not confirmed observing reservations. For clock-independent planning, maximum is about 08:47 UTC; apply the officially announced Moroccan offset nearer the trip. EclipseWise local-circumstances tables.

Eye safety: Use ISO 12312-2 eclipse glasses whenever any bright part of the Sun is visible. Remove them only during totality, at a location actually inside the totality path. Cameras, binoculars, and telescopes need suitable filters over the front of the optics. NASA viewing guidance.

Attractions

  • Kasbah Museum of Mediterranean Cultures {652}: A former sultan’s palace with archaeology, gardens, and exhibits about Tangier’s links across the Mediterranean. Address: Place de la Kasbah, Tangier 90030, Morocco; ساحة القصبة، طنجة ٩٠٠٣٠، المغرب. Hours: 10–6, closed Tu; confirm holiday access. Official museum information | Google Maps.
  • Kasbah Contemporary Art Space {550}: Changing Moroccan and international art exhibitions in the former Kasbah prison, a short walk from the palace museum. Address: Place Al Mechouar / Place de la Kasbah, Tangier 90030, Morocco; ساحة المشور، ساحة القصبة، طنجة ٩٠٠٣٠، المغرب. Hours: 10–6, closed Tu; confirm holiday access. Official museum information | Google Maps.
  • Tangier American Legation Museum {547}: Historic diplomatic rooms, art, and displays on Morocco–US relations in the old city. Address: 8 Rue d’Amérique, Tangier 90000, Morocco; ٨ زنقة أمريكا، طنجة ٩٠٠٠٠، المغرب. Hours: Check current museum opening days and holiday hours. Museum website | Google Maps.
  • Cap Spartel lighthouse and headland {882}: Sea views, lighthouse heritage, gardens, and Atlantic air west of the city. Coastal haze and strong wind are possible; assess the actual morning view before choosing an eclipse site. Address: Route du Cap Spartel, Tangier, Morocco; طريق رأس سبارطيل، طنجة، المغرب. Hours: Visitor complex hours vary seasonally; confirm before departure. Official visitor information | Google Maps.
  • Caves of Hercules {982}: A coastal cave complex near Cap Spartel, known for the shape of its sea opening. The cave interior is a sightseeing stop; eclipse viewing needs an open view of the Sun outside. Address: Route des Grottes d’Hercule, Achakar, Tangier, Morocco; طريق مغارات هرقل، أشقار، طنجة، المغرب. Hours: Daytime visits; check current cave access and closing time. Official destination guide | Google Maps.

Neighborhoods

  • Medina and Petit Socco: Walkable old-city lanes, cafés, and the American Legation. Expect steps, uneven paving, and some steep streets; Petit Socco is mapped as a reference.
  • Kasbah: The hilltop historic quarter around the two museums; compact and rewarding on foot, with a steep approach.
  • Grand Socco and Marshan: Grand Socco is the transition into the medina; the quieter Marshan district farther west has broad views. Both reference points are mapped.
  • Ville Nouvelle / Boulevard Pasteur: Cafés, shops, and the newer city center; the map marks Place de France.
  • Malabata: The eastern bayfront hotel area, useful for coastal accommodation and promenade walks; a different base from the old city.

Do not assume the annual arts season overlaps these dates until the organizer publishes its program. Allow a full day or an overnight stay, and check any proposed eclipse site separately. Address: Place Outa El Hammam, Chefchaouen, Morocco; ساحة وطاء الحمام، شفشاون، المغرب. Hours: Public lanes daily; individual shops and monuments vary. Official destination guide | Google Maps.

It may bring civic decorations and celebrations, but no Tangier parade, concert, or fireworks time is confirmed here. Grand Socco is a central orientation point, not an announced event venue. Address: Place du 9 Avril 1947 (Grand Socco), Tangier, Morocco; ساحة ٩ أبريل ١٩٤٧، السوق الكبير، طنجة، المغرب. Hours: Holiday all day; local program to be announced. Holiday calendar | Google Maps.
  • Total solar eclipse — Monday, August 2 {580}: Tangier receives roughly 4 minutes 50 seconds of totality, with maximum around 08:47 UTC (about 9:47am if Morocco remains on UTC+1). The partial phases run approximately 07:40–10:00 UTC. Reconfirm Morocco’s 2027 clock rules and the exact site’s contact times. The headland is a geographic reference only; no eclipse-day access or booking is implied. Address: Route du Cap Spartel, Tangier, Morocco; طريق رأس سبارطيل، طنجة، المغرب. Hours: Morning; arrive early at the agreed open-air site. Tangier eclipse circumstances | Google Maps.
