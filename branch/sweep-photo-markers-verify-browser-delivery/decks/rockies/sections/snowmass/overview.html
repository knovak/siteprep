Snowmass, Colorado

Tuesday, December 22–Tuesday, December 29, 2026

Basic Info

  • Dates: Tuesday, December 22–Tuesday, December 29, 2026.
  • Place: Snowmass Village, the ski-resort town near Aspen; this section does not refer to Old Snowmass along Highway 82.
  • Population: Town: 3,096 (2020 census). For regional scale, the 2020 Glenwood Springs micropolitan area (Garfield and Pitkin counties, using the 2020 boundaries) had 79,043 residents; this is a micropolitan region, not a separate metropolitan city. Census regional table
  • Altitude and climate: About 8,309 ft (2,533 m); a cold, snowy mountain climate. Late December generally means freezing daytime weather, much colder nights, snowstorms, and strong sun between storms. Allow time to adjust to the altitude.
  • Holidays: Christmas Eve is Thursday, December 24; Christmas Day is Friday, December 25. Kwanzaa runs December 26–January 1; New Year’s Eve is Thursday, December 31, and New Year’s Day is Friday, January 1, within the following week. Reserve holiday meals and verify closures.
  • History: Ranching settlement dates to about 1910; the ski resort opened in 1967, and Snowmass Village incorporated in 1977. The valley is part of the ancestral homeland of the Ute people. Wikipedia history and census
  • Visitor precautions: Secure skis and valuables and use ordinary theft precautions. Current local crime severity has not been verified. Snow, altitude, and icy surfaces require care.

  • Anderson Ranch Arts Center {438}: Contemporary art, rotating gallery exhibitions, working studios, and outdoor sculpture on a historic ranch campus. Address: 5263 Owl Creek Road, Snowmass Village, CO 81615. Hours: October–May M–F 1–5, except major holidays. Official information | Google Maps.
  • The Collective and Base Village rink {513}: A gathering place with an indoor game lounge and seasonal outdoor skating; confirm the winter opening calendar before visiting. Address: 49 Wood Road, Snowmass Village, CO 81615. Hours: winter hours to be confirmed; some spaces close for private events. Official information | Google Maps.

Neighborhoods

  • Base Village: Walkable modern resort center around the gondola, rink, and The Collective at 49 Wood Road.
  • Snowmass Mall: Older pedestrian shopping and dining area uphill from Base Village; use the Sky Cab when operating. Reference: 45 Village Square.
  • Owl Creek / Anderson Ranch: Quieter residential and cultural area; use local transport to reach the arts center in winter.
