Vancouver

Thursday, October 1–Thursday, October 15, 2026

Basic Info

  • Dates: Thursday, October 1–Thursday, October 15, 2026.
  • Population: Vancouver had 662,248 residents in the 2021 census; the Vancouver census metropolitan area had 2,642,825.
  • Altitude and climate: Downtown is near sea level and the city averages about 32 m elevation. Vancouver has a mild oceanic climate; early October typically brings cool, increasingly wet weather, with daytime highs around 14–16°C and nights around 8–10°C. Bring waterproof layers.
  • Holidays: National Day for Truth and Reconciliation is Wednesday, September 30 (the day before arrival), and Thanksgiving is Monday, October 12. Holiday hours and transit schedules may apply.
  • History: This is the unceded traditional territory of the Musqueam, Squamish, and Tsleil-Waututh peoples. The modern city was incorporated on April 6, 1886, after Granville grew around the 1867 Hastings Mill settlement. Read more on Wikipedia.

Attractions

  • Stanley Park: A 405-hectare urban park with seawall, forest trails, beaches, and cultural sites. Address: Stanley Park Drive, Vancouver, BC V6G 1Z4. Hours: Park 6am–10pm; individual facilities vary. Google Maps.
  • Vancouver Art Gallery: The city’s major art museum, with historic and contemporary exhibitions. Address: 750 Hornby Street, Vancouver, BC V6Z 2H7. Hours: hours vary by day; confirm before visiting. Official site | Google Maps.
  • Museum of Vancouver: City-history museum in Vanier Park. Address: 1100 Chestnut Street, Vancouver, BC V6J 3J9. Hours: daily hours vary seasonally. Official site | Google Maps.
  • Granville Island: Walkable arts and food district centered on the Public Market. Address: 1669 Johnston Street, Vancouver, BC V6H 3R9. Hours: island public realm daily; market typically 9am–6pm. Google Maps.

Neighborhoods

  • Downtown and the West End: The most convenient base for galleries, waterfront walks, Stanley Park, and transit.
  • Gastown: Historic streets, independent shops, and restaurants east of Downtown; remain alert around nearby blocks after dark.
  • Yaletown: Walkable warehouse district with restaurants and the seawall.
  • Kitsilano: Beach-oriented neighborhood convenient to Vanier Park and Granville Island.

Vancouver International Film Festival

VIFF 2026 runs October 1–11, fully within the stay. Its global program spans features, documentaries, shorts, talks, industry events, and live performances. The complete film roster and screening times are released at the September 2 program launch; use the official calendar to filter by date, venue, and program and confirm late changes.
