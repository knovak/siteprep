Sunshine Coast, Queensland

Friday, October 16–Saturday, November 21, 2026

Basic Info

  • Dates: Friday, October 16 through Saturday, November 21, 2026.
  • Population: The Sunshine Coast Region local government area had 342,541 residents in the 2021 census; the wider urban area is part of southeast Queensland rather than a separate official metropolitan statistical area.
  • Altitude and climate: Beaches are at sea level while the hinterland rises above 400 m. Late spring is warm and increasingly humid, typically around 25–28°C by day and 16–20°C at night, with strong UV, showers, thunderstorms, and warm ocean water.
  • Holidays: No Queensland public holiday falls during the stay. Halloween (October 31) is not a public holiday; Melbourne Cup Day (November 3) is widely observed socially but is not a Queensland public holiday.
  • History: The region is the Country of the Kabi Kabi and Jinibara peoples. European pastoral settlement expanded from the 1860s; the modern Sunshine Coast Region was formed in 2008. Read more on Wikipedia.
