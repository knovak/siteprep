Westcliffe, Colorado

Tuesday, September 22–Wednesday, September 23, 2026

Basic Info

  • Dates: Tuesday, September 22 through Wednesday, September 23, 2026.
  • Population: Westcliffe had 435 residents in the 2020 census; rural Custer County had 4,704 and is not a metropolitan area.
  • Altitude and climate: The town sits at about 7,888 ft (2,404 m). Late September in the high, semi-arid Wet Mountain Valley often brings mild days around the upper 60s°F (about 20°C), cold nights near freezing, intense sun, and rapidly changing mountain weather.
  • Holidays: No regular U.S. federal or Colorado state holiday falls during the stay or adjacent week. The autumnal equinox is Tuesday, September 22.
  • History: The Ute knew the valley long before settlers arrived. Westcliffe was founded in 1887 as a Denver & Rio Grande Railway terminus and incorporated in 1888. Read more on Wikipedia.

Attractions

  • Bluff Park {418}: A small overlook at the west edge of town with an unobstructed panorama of the Sangre de Cristo range. Address: 110 S Adams Boulevard, Westcliffe, CO 81252. Hours: Open 24h. Official information | Google Maps.
  • Westcliffe Center for the Performing Arts / Jones Theater {763}: A restored historic theater presenting community film and live performance. Address: 119 Main Street, Westcliffe, CO 81252. Hours: Box office and event hours vary. Official information | Google Maps.
  • Silver Cliff Museum {251}: A local-history museum in the former 1879 town hall and firehouse interpreting the mining boom. Address: 606 Main Street, Silver Cliff, CO 81252. Hours: Sa–Su 10–4 seasonally; confirm. Official information | Google Maps.
  • Great Sand Dunes National Park and Preserve {906}: The continent’s tallest dunes rise against the Sangre de Cristo Mountains about a 90-minute drive south. Address: 11999 State Highway 150, Mosca, CO 81146. Hours: Park 24h; visitor center hours vary. Official information | Google Maps.

Events

  • Autumn equinox stargazing, September 22 {584}: Westcliffe and Silver Cliff form an International Dark Sky Community; use the public observing field for a self-guided night-sky session if weather permits. This is an anytime visitor activity rather than a staffed program. Address: Smokey Jack Observatory, 100 S Adams Boulevard, Westcliffe, CO 81252. Hours: After astronomical twilight; observatory programs require a reservation. Official information | Google Maps.
