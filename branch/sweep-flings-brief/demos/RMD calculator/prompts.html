Prompt 1

Model Retirement Growth and Required Withdrawals

Make an interactive tool that models a retirement fund balance under certain controlled conditions. The main question is the growth rate of the assets, and the US federal required minimum withdrawals schedule. Make each of these factors a slider:

  • How many years from present to model
  • How much to start with
  • The age of the person today
  • The asset growth rate

Provide a line graph of the total asset value and of the RMD by year. Also provide those numbers in a tab-separated text table

Prompt 2

Add Target Annual Withdrawals and Documentation

Great. Now let's add one more series of additional annual withdrawals.

Add a slider for "target annual total withdrawals". Calculate an additional annual withdrawal which is this target amount minus the RMD, with a minimum value of zero (i.e. the additional withdrawal can never be negative).

Adjust the calculation to include these withdrawals

write a README document that describes this little model accurately and concisely. Have a short and simple "how to" section, followed by a more complete explanation of how it works
