The deck compares packages that include viewing the 2 August 2027 total solar eclipse. The five topics are classified by the eclipse viewing country/region, not by embarkation airport, headquarters or every country visited. Oosterdam belongs under Tunisia because its planned eclipse viewing is offshore Tunisia. Lake Nasser itineraries can view the eclipse in Luxor or Abydos before sailing.

This is a package comparison across countries, rather than a locality guide. Basic Info covers the common eclipse date, path, August heat and selection rules. Population, founding history, neighborhoods and an unrelated festival/attraction list would not describe this comparison and have not been added. Existing place decks, including Egypt, remain separate.

Evidence needed for inclusion

An exact 2027 departure must publish available places/rooms/cabins, explicitly state available/limited availability, or expose a priced selectable allocation in a public booking flow. A headline price, ordinary enquiry form, registration link, selectable legacy cabin or directory price alone does not establish stock. An explicit sold-out notice overrides a stale selector, catalogue or search snippet. The page describes the evidence for each offer and distinguishes provider-reported allocations from priced booking selections. No booking was made, room held, personal information submitted or operator contacted.

For each included offer, confirm the departure, occupancy restriction, full fare, mandatory extras and eclipse viewing plan. A whole-boat private charter is labelled as such; an available slot is not necessarily suitable for a solo guest. Keep original currencies. Different room and flight allocations can overlap. Do not sum them or treat a deposit as the full price.

The four-person check means four adults in two private double/twin rooms or cabins, two per room, not four berths in one room. Distinguish a published two-room allocation or priced two-room selection from four selectable traveler places. Request forms requiring subsequent availability confirmation remain unconfirmed, even if they calculate a price. For camping/gîte/charter itineraries, state the accommodation mismatch. No bookings, holds, personal-data submissions, operator messages or payments were made during this refresh.

Every main-page description has .package-availability and .party-availability paragraphs; both appear in full in the comparison table. The first records general stock; the second records the strongest evidence for the requested party. Unknown room stock is not sold out.
