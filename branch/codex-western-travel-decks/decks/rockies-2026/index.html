Tips

  • Take the Turquoise Trail to Albuquerque: Follow scenic NM Highway 14 for the longer drive south, stopping in Madrid for its small galleries, shops, and the historic Mine Shaft Tavern, which presents live music on weekends. Nearby Cerrillos is enjoyable for a short drive-through or a lunch stop at Black Bird Saloon; lunch there could make the whole trip worthwhile, but confirm that it is open before setting out. Madrid: Old Coal Town Museum & Mine Shaft Tavern, 2846 NM-14, Madrid, NM 87010. Visit Madrid | Madrid on Google Maps. Cerrillos lunch: Black Bird Saloon, 28 Main Street, Cerrillos, NM 87010; hours vary, so confirm before visiting. Google Maps.
