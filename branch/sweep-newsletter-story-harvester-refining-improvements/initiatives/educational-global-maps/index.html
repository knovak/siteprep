Educational Global Maps

Purpose

2026-08-26

Educational global maps. Provide an interactive environment with many options. These will combine the following.

  • Geographic time series data sets. These may come from our world in data or from the sources for the SOS (science on a sphere) displays.  Anticipate perhaps hundreds in a catalogue.  Accommodate region, country, district, city level resolution.
  • Visualizations. These may map a single data set, or they may combine data sets, for example, with layering or with flows on top of static data.
  • Projections. The visualization could be provided over a conventional map, a Dymaxion map, or possibly a map distorted to be proportional to population.
  • Display. Utilize resolution up to that of a large 4K conventional display. Ideally use AirPlay other wireless connection. Allow the sliders and pan and zoom controls to be off screen, such as on a handheld or laptop device.  Be open to projection onto spherical displays.

Besides the general public, the expected users of this would be high school and undergraduate students.  The plan would be to define interfaces and procedures so that new data sets could be added by others over time.

Done

All eight phases of plan.md are implemented in work/phase-0: 16 source modules behind the canonical scene, catalogue, renderer, temporal, composer, session and sphere contracts, with eight Node test suites and five browser suites (work/phase-0/test). That covers the portable scene core and its ZIP round trip, a metadata-only catalogue with rights-gated contribution adapters, a Canvas reader with Equal Earth, Airocean and a fixed population cartogram, mixed-cadence time with layers, flows and raster frames, immutable educational scenes and portable bundles, a detached controller over a packaged WebSocket relay, teaching and presentation modes at 4K, and a deterministic 2:1 equirectangular frame export with an SOS playlist, labels profile, external legend and checksummed manifest.

Waiting on others

Nothing is owed by a third party. Phase 7 acceptance requires someone other than the implementer to run the contribution, classroom presentation, portable restore and sphere-export exercises, and a real sphere installation and operator if one is available (plan.md Phase 7, test-plan.md §4.7).

Remaining work

Phase 7 is the only open phase. Sphere hardware behaviour is unproved, so the remaining work is to run those four exercises with an independent participant and record the corrections, comprehension problems, preparation effort, performance, and operator burden - then address the findings or carry them as refining work before any output graduates.

Optional later

plan.md §4 defers live provider refresh services, columnar or tiled renderers, local-network relay transport, direct sphere control, student authoring and moderation, variable-driven or subnational cartograms, private catalogues, and collaboration. spec.md also holds out universal provider coverage, a complete historical-boundary ontology, arbitrary contributor code in the browser, real-time collaboration with accounts, and guaranteed offline two-device discovery.
