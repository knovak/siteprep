Basic Info

  • Dates: Tuesday, December 22–Tuesday, December 29, 2026.
  • Population: Town: 6,434 (2020 census). For regional scale, the 2020 Glenwood Springs micropolitan area (Garfield and Pitkin counties, using the 2020 boundaries) had 79,043 residents; this is a micropolitan region, not a separate metropolitan city. Census regional table
  • Altitude and climate: About 6,172 ft (1,881 m), with a dry mountain-valley climate. Expect cold, often snowy December weather, daytime temperatures near freezing and nights well below freezing; trail surfaces can be icy.
  • Holidays: Christmas Eve is Thursday, December 24; Christmas Day is Friday, December 25. Kwanzaa runs December 26–January 1; New Year’s Eve is Thursday, December 31, and New Year’s Day is Friday, January 1, within the following week. Reserve holiday meals and verify closures.
  • History: On Ute homelands, the town developed as a farming and railroad settlement in 1887 and incorporated in 1888; mining and ranching gave way to a broader arts and recreation economy. Wikipedia history and census
  • Visitor precautions: Use ordinary theft precautions in town; lock cars and secure valuables. Current local crime severity has not been verified. Snow and ice can make evening walks and mountain roads difficult.

Neighborhoods

  • Historic downtown / Main Street: The most walkable cluster of restaurants, shops, and art spaces, centered on 4th and Main.
  • Creative District / The Launchpad: Galleries and studios immediately south of Main Street; start at 76 S 4th Street.
  • Sopris Park area: Residential streets and the park just south of downtown; snowbanks and ice can interrupt easy walking.
