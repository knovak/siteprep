Luxor Eclipse

Wednesday, July 28–Saturday, August 7, 2027

Basic Info

  • Dates: Wednesday, July 28 through Saturday, August 7, 2027.
  • Population: Luxor city has roughly 280,000 residents; the wider urban area has about 1.3 million. Population definitions and estimates vary.
  • Altitude and climate: Central Luxor is about 76 m above sea level. The desert climate is extremely hot, dry, and sunny in late July and early August; typical afternoon highs are around 41°C (106°F), with nights around 25°C (77°F). Plan outdoor visits near opening time, carry water, and use shade and sun protection.
  • Holidays: Revolution Day is Friday, July 23, five days before arrival. It is a national public holiday and can affect transport and opening schedules during the adjacent week. No regular national or major Islamic holiday is expected during the stay itself; lunar-calendar dates can shift with official observation.
  • Safety: Crime is generally a moderate concern for visitors, with scams, persistent selling, and petty theft more likely than violent crime. Use licensed transport, secure valuables, and follow current government travel advice.
  • History: Luxor occupies part of ancient Thebes, which rose to prominence around 2055 BCE and became a capital of Egypt during the Middle and New Kingdoms. The modern city developed around Luxor Temple and the Nile. Read more on Wikipedia.

Solar Eclipse — August 2, 2027

Luxor is inside the path of totality on Monday, August 2. Totality is expected shortly after 1pm local time and should last about 6 minutes 20 seconds near Luxor, close to the eclipse’s maximum duration. The Sun will be high in the sky, so select an open site with shade, water, toilets, controlled access, and an unobstructed view well before the trip.

Eye safety: Use certified ISO 12312-2 eclipse viewers whenever any part of the bright Sun is visible. Remove them only during totality, when the Sun is completely covered, and put them back on as soon as the bright photosphere returns. Never look through a camera, binoculars, or telescope without a securely mounted solar filter on the front of the optics. Recheck contact times, Egypt’s 2027 clock rules, local permits, transport closures, and the viewing site shortly before travel.

Attractions

  • Karnak Temple Complex {742}: A vast ceremonial complex developed over roughly two millennia, best known for the Great Hypostyle Hall. Address: Karnak, Luxor, Luxor Governorate, Egypt; الكرنك، الأقصر، محافظة الأقصر، مصر. Hours: Daily, generally early morning–5; confirm seasonal hours. Official information | Google Maps.
  • Luxor Temple {315}: A New Kingdom temple on the East Bank, linked to Karnak by the restored Avenue of Sphinxes and especially atmospheric after sunset. Address: Corniche El Nil, Luxor, Luxor Governorate, Egypt; كورنيش النيل، الأقصر، محافظة الأقصر، مصر. Hours: Daily, generally 6am–8pm; confirm seasonal hours. Official information | Google Maps.
  • Valley of the Kings {891}: The royal necropolis contains decorated New Kingdom tombs, including Tutankhamun’s; the standard ticket covers a changing selection. Address: West Bank, Luxor, Luxor Governorate, Egypt; البر الغربي، الأقصر، محافظة الأقصر، مصر. Hours: Daily, generally 6–5; arrive at opening in summer. Official information | Google Maps.
  • Mortuary Temple of Hatshepsut {467}: Terraced colonnades rise against the cliffs at Deir el-Bahari at the memorial temple of the 18th-dynasty pharaoh. Address: Deir el-Bahari, West Bank, Luxor, Egypt; الدير البحري، البر الغربي، الأقصر، مصر. Hours: Daily, generally 6–5; arrive at opening in summer. Official information | Google Maps.
  • Luxor Museum {628}: A compact archaeological museum with carefully presented sculpture and objects from Thebes, useful for understanding the surrounding sites. Address: Corniche El Nil, Luxor, Luxor Governorate, Egypt; كورنيش النيل، الأقصر، محافظة الأقصر، مصر. Hours: Daily with morning and evening sessions; confirm current summer hours. Official information | Google Maps.
  • Medinet Habu {154}: Ramesses III’s large, well-preserved memorial temple has vivid reliefs and substantial enclosure walls. Address: Al Bairat, West Bank, Luxor, Egypt; البعيرات، البر الغربي، الأقصر، مصر. Hours: Daily, generally 6–5; arrive early. Official information | Google Maps.

Neighborhoods

  • East Bank Corniche and Downtown: The walkable visitor center around Luxor Temple, the Nile promenade, hotels, restaurants, and the ferry landing; the map pin marks Luxor Temple.
  • Karnak: The district north of downtown surrounds the Karnak complex and its visitor facilities; the map pin marks the main entrance.
  • Gezira: A quieter West Bank village around the public-ferry landing, with guesthouses and easy access to fields and archaeological sites.
  • Al Bairat: The dispersed West Bank base closest to Medinet Habu, the Colossi of Memnon, and roads to the royal tombs.

Address: Karnak Temple, Karnak, Luxor, Egypt; معبد الكرنك، الكرنك، الأقصر، مصر. Hours: Nightly schedule varies; reserve and reconfirm. Official schedule | Google Maps.

Location: Typical launch area, Al Bairat, West Bank, Luxor, Egypt; البعيرات، البر الغربي، الأقصر، مصر. Hours: Hotel pickup before dawn; flight times and launch sites vary. Google Maps.
