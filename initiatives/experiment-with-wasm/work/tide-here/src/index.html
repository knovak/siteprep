What travels with this file?

The entire tide model used here. All 65,203 coastal points and the place catalogue are embedded in this HTML file. Clearing browser storage does not remove them. The model calculates dates as needed; it is not a table that runs out at the end of the year.

This edition uses the global FES2022 model everywhere. The online Tide Here can use national-provider predictions; this offline edition cannot fetch those services. Inland locations, unresolved harbours, and coasts with no model point within 40 km return a coverage message.

Heights are in metres relative to the model’s mean sea level, not chart datum. Weather, storm surge, waves and river flow are excluded. Future changes to coasts, time-zone rules and the tide model require an updated app file.

Place search covers 170,946 named places and their aliases from GeoNames. It is a catalogue, not an address-search service. If a beach or small island is missing, enter its coordinates. “Show here” asks your browser for location permission; your operating system may use location services.

Searches and forecasts are calculated on your device. The app makes no network requests. Links below open external sources only when you choose them.

FES2022 Tide product funded by CNES and produced by LEGOS, NOVELTIS and CLS, made available by AVISO. Tide Here transformed the native atlas into sampled coastal harmonics; this edition packs those constants for browser use. FES2022 source · AVISO licence.

Names and coordinates: GeoNames, CC BY 4.0, selected and compacted September 7, 2026. Coastline: Natural Earth (public domain); time-zone assignments derived from timezone-boundary-builder, ODbL. Sun and moon: SunCalc 2.0.1.
