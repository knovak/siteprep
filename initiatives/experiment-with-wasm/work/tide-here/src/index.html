Data sources and station options

Show tides searches places online, then looks for official US or Canadian predictions. A station within 25 km is selected automatically when it is clearly closer than the next one. Otherwise, choose a station below. If online lookup is unavailable or has no supported station, the app explains why it is using its bundled model.

Place searches go to Photon / OpenStreetMap. Tide requests go directly to NOAA and the Canadian Hydrographic Service. Saved responses reduce repeat downloads. Select Local model only to prevent online requests from the normal search.

What travels with this file?

The entire tide model used here. All 65,203 coastal points and the place catalogue are embedded in this HTML file. Clearing browser storage does not remove them. The model calculates dates as needed; it is not a table that runs out at the end of the year.

Official NOAA and Canadian station predictions are preferred when available, with their own labelled height datums. The included FES2022 model supplies the worldwide fallback. Inland locations, unresolved harbours, and coasts with no model point within 40 km return a coverage message.

The source and height datum are shown with every forecast. Model heights are relative to mean sea level; official stations use their published chart datum. Weather, storm surge, waves and river flow are excluded. Future changes to coasts, time-zone rules and the tide model require an updated app file.

Normal place search uses Photon / OpenStreetMap for addresses, beaches and small places. The offline fallback covers 170,946 named places and their aliases from GeoNames. If a beach or small island is missing, enter its coordinates. “Show here” asks your browser for location permission; your operating system may use location services.

The included catalogue and model run on your device. Show tides and the other forecast entry points try online data first, with saved responses and source labels. Local model only uses no internet. Links below open external sources when you choose them.
