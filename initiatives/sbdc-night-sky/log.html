2026-11-15 — Beta upgrade, unit 2: settings and presets for the sdross0 setups

Unit 1 merged as knovak/siteprep#652 and the site build published it to the beta.

Unit 2 adds the settings needed to reproduce the other simulator's setups: latitude to a tenth of a degree, the 54 filed Sun-synchronous levels as a shell option, an "as filed" plane layout with two planes per level and evenly spaced satellites, the plane spread, the scatter in brightness between satellites, and the measured Starlink Gen2 Mini phase curve beside the Lambertian sphere. The SBDC brightness slider now moves in steps of 0.1. Each default is the earlier behaviour: test/golden/golden.json and the Site 1 counts of the paired fixture are unchanged.

Three presets set that project's constellation with its two brightness models, and a "Copy link" button writes the settings into the page address. The three place presets now use the places' latitudes to a tenth of a degree.

With the new settings the picture shows distinct bands, as the other simulator's does. For the documented cases with a dark sky the no-mitigation count is 0.85 to 1.00 of the other simulator's, and the December optimistic count is 0.98 of it. In the two twilight cases this simulator counts three to four times as many faint satellites; that is the twilight-table fault, which unit 3 corrects. beta/PLAN.md has the table.

113 tests pass in beta/, and the browser spec has ten. lib/, work/ and demos/SBDC Night Sky/ are unchanged.

2026-11-15 — Beta upgrade, unit 3: the twilight sky, ambient light, the threshold

Unit 2 merged as knovak/siteprep#653 and the site build published it to the beta.

Unit 3 corrects the twilight sky-brightness table to the V-band measurements of Patat et al. (2006), which the table cited and did not match: it was 3.2 magnitudes too dark with the Sun 6° down. The naked-eye limit at the end of civil twilight goes from 3.8 to 1.5. The opening view, 30 minutes after sunset, now shows 546 visible SBDCs and 2 stars where it showed 3,290 and 67; from an hour after sunset the counts are within about 15% of what they were. test/golden/golden.json and the Site 1 counts of the paired fixture were regenerated, and the fixture keeps the documented counts beside the new ones. The sky color ramp's anchors moved with the table.

Added ambient light by Bortle class, 1 to 9, which brightens the sky and lowers the limit, and a choice of naked-eye threshold: the simulator's table, or the Schaefer closed form the other simulator uses. The sdross0 presets use the second. The readout gives the zenith sky brightness and the limit.

With those settings the no-mitigation counts in the two twilight cases are 1.00 and 1.04 of the other simulator's (1.11 and 1.17 before), and the optimistic counts 1.8 and 1.25 of it (3.9 and 3.1 before). With the Schaefer threshold and the Bortle table this simulator reproduces the limits the other project publishes for five Bortle classes to within 0.03 magnitude.

125 tests pass in beta/, and the browser spec has thirteen. lib/, work/ and demos/SBDC Night Sky/ are unchanged.

2026-11-15 — Beta upgrade, unit 4: the Moon, moonlight, light ratios

Unit 3 merged as knovak/siteprep#654 and the site build published it to the beta.

Unit 4 adds the Moon: off by default, as on a date in a chosen year, or at a chosen phase. Its place comes from the Astronomical Almanac's low-precision series, which the tests check against three eclipses. A Moon above the horizon lights the sky by the model of Krisciunas and Schaefer (1991), which lowers the limit and hides faint satellites and stars. The Moon is drawn with its phase.

The readout has a new line with the summed light of all sunlit SBDCs above the horizon and its ratio to a full Moon, to the Moon as set, and to a moonless natural night sky. With the default constellation at 40° N on a December evening the SBDC light is about an eighth of a full Moon's and 27 times the natural night sky's; at midnight in June it is 1/758 and about a third.

Two faults corrected. Turning the view in the instant after a rebuild could raise "Simulator error", because the picture paired the new constellation with the old evaluation; this was in the simulator before the upgrade. And the readout strip covered the horizon; it is now below the sky.

test/golden/golden.json gained the light block; no number already in it changed. 137 tests pass in beta/, and the browser spec has eighteen. lib/, work/ and demos/SBDC Night Sky/ are unchanged.

2026-11-15 — Beta upgrade, unit 5: hours of significant light, perspective view, aiming

Unit 4 merged as knovak/siteprep#655 and the site build published it to the beta.

Unit 5 adds a "Night & year" group. For the chosen latitude and date it counts the hours in which SBDC light is significant, under three definitions side by side: at least 100 SBDCs visible to the naked eye, visible SBDCs outnumbering visible stars, and SBDC light exceeding the sky's own light. It does the same for a year from 24 sampled dates, and draws visible SBDCs and stars through the night. Moonlight and ambient light are included when they are on.

With the default constellation at 40° N, at least 100 SBDCs are visible for 8.3 of the 8.9 hours of a June night and for 4,011 of a year's 4,304 night hours. With the sdross0 optimistic-mitigation settings at 37.2° N the figure is 1.5 hours on September 22 and 597 hours a year. The sdross0 hours match the durations that project states.

Also added: a perspective view, which draws the sky as a camera records it and is the other simulator's projection, and an "Aim at densest sky" button. beta/README.md has a short guide to everything the upgrade added.

148 tests pass in beta/, and the browser spec has twenty-two. lib/, work/ and demos/SBDC Night Sky/ are unchanged. This is the last of the five units in beta/PLAN.md.

2026-11-16 — Beta: the page fits a phone again

Checking the sticky sky at phone size (iPhone 13, 390 px) found that the beta page was 1,452 px wide, so the phone shrank it to fit and the controls were too small to use. Unit 4 moved the readout strip out from over the sky and into the page's flow; its lines do not wrap on a narrow screen, and the grid and flex containers around it took their width from the longest line. The columns now take the screen's width, and a long line is cut with an ellipsis. The released simulator never had this fault. At that width the credit line takes two lines and covered the compass points along the horizon, so on a narrow screen it is now at the top of the readout strip, below the sky.

beta/PLAN.md lists it as fault 12, and the browser spec has a test at 390 × 844. The sticky sky itself is unchanged: on a phone the sky still scrolls away in both the released simulator and the beta, and it stays the mobile-sky-follow-up todo item.
