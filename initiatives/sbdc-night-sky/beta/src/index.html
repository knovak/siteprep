Assumptions & data

Everything the simulation believes, in one auditable table. Rendered live from the embedded sbdc-data.json block — replace that block to change the physics without touching code. Confidence: measured published modeled assumed. The photometry section is the least certain — the FCC filing gives no satellite sizes or albedos; the renders are illustrative, not photometric predictions.
