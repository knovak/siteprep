# Wish

## 2026-09-09

I would like to start a new initiative to produce a web-based manager for “flings”. A fling is a series of one or more social events. A fling could be as simple as a movie followed by a meal, or more complicated like a series of gatherings across a weekend for a wedding, or something larger like a set of concerts over a long period.

Here’s a picture of the information that needs to be managed. First there are organizers who invite members and create activities. Activities consist of events which have location and time, and often require polls of the membership or coordinated payments among the members. Flings, activities and events can all have discussions where members exchange messages. In addition, organizers can use AI agents to draft direct email or text messages to particular members or sub groups within a fling.

The website should allow for multiple flings and multiple organizers, with flings, potentially having more than one organizer. An organizer will have a user interface that members don’t see, to create or close flings, to invite members, to create activities, etc.. An organizer can also have a view of the fling from the point of view of any member, to feel more confident about what members experience.

Members will have a destination page for their membership in a fling, which will summarize all the information they need. There will be information about every activity to which they’ve been invited, and additional information available about activities within the fling where they have accepted an invitation.

Flings will be very independent from one another. A member will have to access a different page for each fling.

Members can get access to a fling page with a URL that contains a code unique to them in the fling. Login will not be necessary.

The organizer interface will support organizers in sending out direct messages. Messages may contain updates about the events, or about discussions, or other instructions from the organizer. Sometimes a message will both be sent directly and recorded as part of a discussion.
