# Background

Researched 2026-08-18, before objectives were drafted. Findings only.

## Already being provided

### [BioDigital Human](https://www.biodigital.com/product/the-biodigital-human)

BioDigital provides browser-based, embeddable 3D anatomy with more than 14,000 individual structures, male and female anatomy, and tools for authoring guided views. Its current Personal plan is free but limited to Anatomy & Physiology content, ten model views per month, and five saved models; School and Business pricing requires contacting sales. Its published product scope is anatomy, physiology, disease, and treatment rather than a personalized practice library for Feldenkrais, yoga, and Alexander Technique movements. ([Product](https://www.biodigital.com/product/the-biodigital-human); [May 2026 plan changes](https://support.biodigital.com/hc/en-us/articles/39657133675927-May-2026-Changes-to-Individual-Plans-and-Free-Trials))

### [Muscle & Motion Yoga](https://www.muscleandmotion.com/yoga-functional-anatomy-app/)

Muscle & Motion combines real-person demonstrations with 3D models that identify contracting and lengthening muscles, joint actions, misalignments, modifications, and preparatory exercises for yoga poses. The advertised annual plan is $7.42 per month billed annually, with tax potentially added. Its published catalog covers yoga rather than Feldenkrais or Alexander Technique, and describes a standard advanced 3D model rather than a body shaped from an individual user's measurements. ([Feature guide](https://www.muscleandmotion.com/knowledge-center/yoga-app/); [pricing](https://www.muscleandmotion.com/yoga-functional-anatomy-app/))

### [ZygoteBody](https://www.zygotebody.com/welcome)

ZygoteBody is an interactive browser anatomy explorer with navigation, a 3D structure hierarchy, dissection, notes, and the ability to isolate muscle-and-bone combinations. It advertises free access and a Premium tier without publishing the Premium price on its welcome page. Its described feature set is anatomical exploration rather than a cross-discipline movement collection or an individualized visual twin. ([Welcome and feature page](https://www.zygotebody.com/welcome); [viewer](https://www.zygotebody.com/))

### [OpenSim](https://opensim.stanford.edu/)

OpenSim is free, open-source musculoskeletal modeling and simulation software used by researchers to study movement and estimate quantities such as muscle forces and joint loads. It supplies the simulation side of the wish, but its public offering is a research toolkit and model repository rather than a guided consumer website or a curated somatic-movement library. ([OpenSim community](https://opensim.stanford.edu/); [subject-specific simulation example](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38347085/))

### [SMPL and SKEL](https://smpl.is.tue.mpg.de/)

SMPL is a poseable human surface model learned from thousands of body scans, available in male, female, and gender-neutral forms with up to 300 shape components; the related SKEL model joins an SMPL-compatible skin surface to a biomechanical skeleton. Access to the full models requires registration and agreement to their licenses, while the separately defined SMPL-Body asset format is available under CC BY 4.0. The published descriptions cover skin, pose, shape, and skeleton, not a complete animated muscle layer or the requested movement corpus. ([SMPL](https://smpl.is.tue.mpg.de/); [SMPL-Body license](https://smpl.is.tue.mpg.de/bodylicense.html))

### [Open 3D anatomy sources](https://anatomytool.org/open3dmodel)

AnatomyTOOL is developing a free human anatomy model under CC BY-SA, and its associated browser viewer code is offered under GPL 3.0. The US National Library of Medicine's Visible Human datasets are public-domain anatomical image data; a University of Denver derivative provides 260 CC BY 4.0 lower-extremity geometries, including muscles, bones, cartilage, ligaments, and fat from one male and one female specimen. These sources reduce the licensing barrier for anatomical assets, but the cited datasets do not themselves provide a whole-body, shape-adjustable, movement-ready visual twin. ([AnatomyTOOL model](https://anatomytool.org/open3dmodel); [viewer](https://caskanatomy.info/open3dviewer/?export=on&model=overview_demo&subset=lessbonesnomuscle); [Visible Human Project](https://www.nlm.nih.gov/research/visible/visible_human.html); [lower-extremity geometries](https://digitalcommons.du.edu/visiblehuman/))

The closest published offerings split the wish across layered anatomy, yoga biomechanics, parametric body surfaces, and research-grade musculoskeletal simulation; none of the sources above describes the complete combination of all four with Feldenkrais and Alexander Technique content in a public website. ([BioDigital](https://www.biodigital.com/product/the-biodigital-human); [Muscle & Motion](https://www.muscleandmotion.com/yoga-functional-anatomy-app/); [SMPL](https://smpl.is.tue.mpg.de/); [OpenSim](https://opensim.stanford.edu/))

## Lessons from similar attempts

### The movement traditions are not interchangeable posture catalogs

Feldenkrais Awareness Through Movement lessons emphasize how a movement is explored and perceived, not merely the movement's endpoint. Alexander Technique lessons use verbal and manual guidance to change habitual responses during simple activities such as sitting, standing, walking, speaking, and reaching. A visualization that records only joint trajectories would therefore represent less than the practices' published teaching descriptions. ([Feldenkrais Network International](https://www.feldenkraisinternational.org/en/feldenkrais-method/range-of-application/awareness-through-movement/); [American Society for the Alexander Technique](https://alexandertechniqueusa.org/faq/))

### Movement-source rights need separate treatment from anatomy rights

The Feldenkrais Guild of North America lists Feldenkrais, Awareness Through Movement, and related terms as protected marks, and the International Feldenkrais Federation states that its archived lessons may not be copied or posted online without permission from the rights holder. The UK Guild's free audio lesson archive likewise marks those lessons as non-commercial, while the Society of Teachers of the Alexander Technique restricts republication of its website text and graphics. Open anatomical data does not remove these separate content and branding constraints. ([FGNA service marks](https://www.feldenkraisguild.com/page/service-mark-use); [IFF copyright information](https://feldenkrais-method.org/media/xrkh0jnd/iff-copyright-information.pdf); [UK Guild lessons](https://www.feldenkrais.co.uk/explore/listen-to-lessons/); [STAT copyright statement](https://alexandertechnique.co.uk/content/copyright-statement))

### Personal appearance is easier to parameterize than internal anatomy

SMPL can vary visible body shape using learned parameters, but subject-specific musculoskeletal studies use medical images and 3D motion-capture data to personalize internal models. Research comparing generic scaled models with more extensively personalized ones reports differences in estimated biomechanical outputs. Weight, gender, and surface shape alone therefore do not establish that the displayed muscles and internal mechanics are specific to an individual. ([SMPL](https://smpl.is.tue.mpg.de/); [subject-specific coordination study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38347085/); [personalized knee-model framework](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0021929024004652))

### Evidence and safety claims require narrow wording

A 2015 systematic review of Feldenkrais trials found heterogeneous studies and high risk of bias, while a 2022 review found positive results in some populations and outcomes across sixteen included studies. A 2012 Alexander Technique review found strong evidence for chronic back pain and moderate evidence for Parkinson's-associated disability but insufficient evidence for several other uses. A 2026 review of published yoga adverse-event reports found that adverse events were uncommon but could be serious, particularly with underlying risk factors. These records do not support treating every visualized movement as universally therapeutic or risk-free. ([Feldenkrais 2015 review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4408630/); [Feldenkrais 2022 review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36360614/); [Alexander Technique review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22171910/); [yoga adverse-event review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42471295/))

### Browser delivery exists, but content reuse is not automatically licensed

BioDigital demonstrates that complex anatomy can be delivered and embedded using web standards, and AnatomyTOOL's viewer demonstrates an open browser-based route. BioDigital separately limits publication and commercial use of captured images and video, showing that access to a viewer does not imply a right to republish its anatomical content in another public site. ([BioDigital product](https://www.biodigital.com/product/the-biodigital-human); [BioDigital media-use policy](https://support.biodigital.com/hc/en-us/articles/1500001842741-BioDigital-s-Image-and-Video-Use-Policy); [AnatomyTOOL viewer](https://caskanatomy.info/open3dviewer/?export=on&model=overview_demo&subset=lessbonesnomuscle))

## Questions this raises

- Does "visual twin" mean a recognizable surface avatar, an anatomically personalized internal model, or an explicit approximation with user-adjustable proportions?
- Is the experience meant to demonstrate movements, guide a person while they practice, compare the person with the model, or support all three?
- Which lesson descriptions and recordings can be licensed for a public and potentially commercial movement library?
- How will the visual language preserve attention, ease, inhibition, and sensory inquiry when those are central to the source practice but not directly visible in a joint animation?
- What level of anatomical and biomechanical accuracy must each visualization claim, and who validates it?
- Which movements or users require screening, contraindications, modifications, or an instructor rather than self-guided use?
- Which body attributes are useful controls, and which terms describe anatomy without treating gender as a proxy for every internal difference?
