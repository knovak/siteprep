This is a personal software workshop that mostly runs itself. Agents do the
work, and a defined lifecycle keeps track of what the work is for.

Three kinds of content live here side by side, and the working instructions keep
their vocabularies apart on purpose.

@figure content-areas

Initiatives are where the software gets made, and what it gets made for is
usually a deck or a demo. Here's the pattern I built this around: an idea shows up
half-formed, I work on it for an afternoon, and then it waits — sometimes for
months. That only works because of the record. It holds the original intent, the
reasoning that came after it, and the next useful move, all in one place, so
picking the work back up costs a read instead of an evening spent reconstructing
what I was thinking.
[The working conventions are authoritative here](source:AGENTS.md), and
[the design and its reasoning live in the vision document](source:INITIATIVES_VISION.md).
