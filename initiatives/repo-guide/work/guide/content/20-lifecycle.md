An initiative is a durable unit of intent. No expiry date, no owner it has to be
handed off to, no obligation to finish. What it accumulates instead is
reasoning.

@figure lifecycle-flow

Movement runs both ways: an initiative that hits a bad assumption moves back,
and one nobody needs right now goes quiet without anyone declaring it dead.

Each move records one more piece of the thinking. First the person's own words,
untouched. Then a statement of what done would mean. Then a chosen shape, with
the alternatives that lost written down next to it. Then an order for the work
and the tests that would show it works.

@fact lifecycle.stage_documents as stack

Those are only the part of the record the lifecycle gates. Several more
documents fill in around them, and none of them waits for a stage to arrive.
The goal in the person's own words is fixed the moment the initiative merges,
amended in place afterwards rather than rewritten. The decisions file holds the
questions the work got stuck on and how each was settled, dated and appended, so
a settled argument does not get had again in November. The log says what
happened and when — routine entries a decision would be buried among, which is
why the two are separate files. Notes keep the maybes nobody has committed to,
and a release writes its own record.

## An initiative keeps intent alive

Not a project with a deadline. It's a durable unit of intent, and it can
advance, move back when an assumption breaks, or rest until the next version is
worth making. Nothing gets deleted on the way through.

## Each move adds to the record

Each move records one more piece of the thinking: purpose, then outcomes, then a
chosen shape with the alternatives that lost, then an order for making and
testing it.

## The record is wider than the stages

The lifecycle gates only some of it. The goal in the person's own words, the
questions the work got stuck on and how each was settled, and the log of what
happened are all part of the record too, and none of them waits for a stage to
arrive.
