2026-09-02

I want you to take a fresh look at the repo guide initiative. The version that exists now is adequate. But its use of text is poor and it could use a better appearance and we need to check that it completely covers the lifecycle and products of an initiative.

So the objective of this work would be a new and better repo Guide, especially for the document and the deck. You could also make a list of how you would change the simulator, but don't spend much time on that yet.

Here are some good points about the first version of the Guide.

  • Just one slide or page near the end for each of the decks and demos
  • Some graphics for the processing products. I think these graphics could be improved, and more graphics in general would help
  • It has information for someone who might want to fork the repository

Here are some weaknesses of the first version of the Guide:

  • No mention of deployment, to either demos or ChatGPT sites
  • No mention of the background on the wish, or the critique phase on the plan
  • Too much abstract language, and too many rhetorical flourishes. I already took a manual pass through it, with mixed results. So I suggest you just start over, and avoid all mannered prose.
  • Inconsistent voice. The reader of the document should be a developer learning about it for the first time, and considering making their own use of it. This is less about how the repo manages itself, and more about what a developer could expect from it.

This version supersedes the deliverable list below. The earlier wording remains visible as the historical record.
